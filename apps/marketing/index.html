1.0Write once.
Post free.

A click posts through your own session at no cost. The queue posts on time through the X API, priced on the button.

  • A queue that keeps time

    Slots from your best hours. Drafts post on schedule, even with the app closed.

  • A viral library

    Daily picks for your keywords, X's Radar and top posts, and every post your session saw.

  • Assist in your voice

    Tighten, a stronger hook, three tones to pick from. It runs on your own model login.

2.0Reply where
it counts.

Find the people worth knowing, keep them in lists, and let rules handle the routine through the API, each with a cap.

  • Engagement targets

    Posts for your keywords from what your session already saw. A reply you click is free.

  • Lead lists

    People in lists and stages, with signal agents that fill them on a schedule.

  • Automations

    When, only if, then. Every rule has a daily cap and a budget, and never touches your session.

3.0Know what
worked.

Views, followers and top posts from what your session saw, free. A refresh reads the full timeline, priced.

  • Best time

    The hours your posts drew views, from posts your session saw.

  • Every post's numbers

    Views, likes and replies per post, plus a nightly private-metrics read once you start it.

  • Account scores

    A score and a tier for any handle, from Noted to Supreme, with who moved it.

4.0One service,
every surface.

Every click is one service method. The app, a browser, the CLI and MCP call the same one, and cost and log the same.

xtend
$ xtend post "Shipping log, week 45"   # post now, priced
$ xtend queue                       # drafts waiting
$ xtend report @lenap               # a stored score
$ xtend spend                       # month against budget
$ xtend mcp                         # tools for any agent
$ xtend service install             # runs with the app closed

  • Desktop

    macOS, Windows and Linux, on Apple silicon, Intel and Arm.

  • Browser

    Open the tools against a running service from any machine you own.

  • Remote service

    Run it on a Mac mini or a VPS so the queue fires with the laptop shut.
