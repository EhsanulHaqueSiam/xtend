# Plan

Plan is when things post: a week calendar, the queue, and the posting slots that drive it.
Everything here is in the time zone from Settings, never the browser's.

## Calendar

The week as seven columns. Each day lists its posts and its open slots by time: queued drafts on
their slot, posted ones with their status dot, and empty slots still ahead. An empty slot takes
a draft from its menu. The header counts the empty slots left this week as a nudge to fill
them. Suggested drafts from Library and Automate sit above the week, each one click from the
next free slot.

## Queue

Queued and failed drafts by time. A row opens the draft in Compose, where it can be unqueued.
**Post now** confirms with the treg price before it charges.

## Slots

The posting slots as a table: one time per row with the weekdays it applies to, saved
together. A queued draft takes the next slot that is free. Each slot needs at least one day.

Under the table, **Best hours** lists the hours where your observed posts drew the most views,
each one click from becoming a slot. It is built from the posts the session has seen, so it is
free; refresh your posts in [Analyze](./analyze.md) to make it sharper.
