# Leads

People who might become customers, and the agents that find them.

## People

Everyone you added, with a stage: new, contacted, replied, customer or ignored. A person's
detail shows their profile, your notes, their latest post with a reply box, and a DM box.
Draft DM and the reply draft are paid model calls; sending goes through X's API at its price.

Add a person by handle, from a post's author, from New followers, or by dragging a post onto
Leads in the sidebar.

## Lists

Lists group people. A list's criteria (bio contains, minimum followers, follow ratio, country,
not in another list) preview who would match before you add them all.

## Agents

Signal agents are saved searches the service runs on a schedule, such as "Typefully
switchers", and files the matches into People. Each has a query, a cadence, an optional note on
what makes a match worth your time, and a budget. Suggested agents proposes some from your
keywords. Each run is paid and logged with what it found and spent.

## New followers

Who followed you since the last check, a page at a time. Each page is a paid read. Add a
follower to People from the row.
