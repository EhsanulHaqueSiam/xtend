# Analyze

Analyze is your own numbers. One paid **Refresh** reads your profile and up to 100 of your own
posts; every section then reads from that load, and the header says how many posts it holds
and when. Until the first refresh each section shows one line and the button.

## Overview

Followers by day, views by day published over 30 days, engagements over views, and the
headline numbers with their change.

## Posts

Your posts as a sortable table with averages by type (text, image, video, link, thread), and
the posts that gained followers. **Private metrics** (profile visits, link clicks, detail
expands) are X's own analytics, read by a nightly job once per own post of the last 30 days;
the section shows the last run and its price.

## Audience

Who you see most, from the session's observations, and your followers over time.

**Themes** groups your best 50 posts by what they are about and says why each group works.
It is one model call, cached per set of posts: asking again for the same posts is free.

## Best time

A 7x24 grid of average views per hour of week in your time zone. Opacity follows the value.
It needs refreshed posts; Plan's Slots turn its best hours into posting slots.

## Competitors

Watched handles side by side with you: followers, posting rate, average views. A row opens
their top posts. Watching is free; the list is shared with Scores' Watchlist.

## Ask

One question, one answer, over the loaded posts summary. Suggested questions fill the box;
asking is a separate, priced click. It is not a chat: the last answer stays until the next
question.
