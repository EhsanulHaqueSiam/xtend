# Analyze

How your account is doing.

## Where the numbers come from

Your session is the free source: every post of yours that scrolls past, every profile visit,
is recorded. Refresh posts is the paid one: it reads your profile and up to 100 of your posts
through X's API. The page header says which fed the page and when. The Refresh button only
turns solid when the data is missing or a day old.

## Sections

- **Overview**: followers, 30 day change, following, posts; activity windows; views by day
  published; followers by day.
- **Posts**: every fetched post with its numbers, sortable. Private metrics (impressions and
  profile clicks) appear when the session saw them.
- **Audience**: who engages most, and themes in your best 50 posts, grouped by what they are
  about and why each works (a paid model call, cached per set of posts).
- **Best time**: average views by hour of week, in your time zone. A cell is one click from a
  Plan slot.
- **Competitors**: handles to watch, each fetched at its own price.
- **Ask**: a question about your posts and audience, answered from the fetched posts. Asking
  again about the same posts is free.
