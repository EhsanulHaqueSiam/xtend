# Analyze

Analyze is your own numbers. Every post of yours the session sees on X (your profile, your
timeline, a thread) lands here with its numbers, free, and the header says "live from your
session". One paid **Refresh posts** reads your profile and up to 100 of your posts for a fuller
picture and your follower count; a post seen after the refresh keeps its newer numbers. The
button only stands out when nothing is loaded or the refresh is a day old.

In the desktop app, **Profile on X** opens your profile in the X panel for free, which is also
how your posts arrive without a refresh.

## Overview

Followers by day, views by day published over 30 days, engagements over views, and the
headline numbers with their change. Move over a history chart to read a date and its value,
or focus it and use the arrow keys. Home and End jump to the first and last sample. The
vertical range fits the data, including score histories in Scores.

**Ask**, at the bottom, takes one question and gives one answer over the loaded posts summary.
Suggested questions fill the box; asking is a separate, priced click. It is not a chat: the
last answer stays until the next question.

## Posts

Your posts as a sortable table with averages by type (text, image, video, link, thread), and
the posts that gained followers. **Private metrics** (profile visits, link clicks, detail
expands) are X's own analytics, read once per own post of the last 30 days. **Fetch now** shows
its price; your first one also turns on a nightly read at 03:00 at the same price, which a switch
beside it turns off. The section
shows the last run.

**Themes**, under the table, groups your best 50 posts by what they are about and says why each group works.
It is one model call, cached per set of posts: asking again for the same posts is free.

## Audience

Followers gained since the last check. One check reads one followers page of your handle,
priced up front; a check under 10 minutes old serves free. A follower is in Leads › People
once you add them. Who fills your feed is in Library › Seen, under **Authors**.

## Best time

A 7x24 grid of average views per hour of week in your time zone. Opacity follows the value.
It reads the posts above, seen or refreshed; Plan's Slots turn its best hours into posting slots.

## Competitors

Watched handles side by side with you: followers, posting rate, average views. Before a fetch,
followers and average views come free from their posts your session saw, marked seen. A row opens
their top posts. Watching is free; the list is shared with Scores' Watchlist. Each row has
**Profile on X**, free, beside **Fetch**, the paid read that fills the row, with its price.

## X's own analytics

**X overview**, **X content**, **X video** and **X audience** are X's own account analytics
(x.com's Analytics in Creator Studio), read from X's page through your session in the desktop
app, free. X shows them only to Premium accounts: without Premium these sections show X's
paywall, and **Upgrade** opens X's own Premium page.

- **X overview**: X's numbers for the range with their change on the range before, and one of
  them by day as bars or a line. X's ranges are 7D, 2W, 4W, 3M and 1Y; X shows them, and the
  numbers beside followers, once you have 50 followers.
- **X content**: your posts in the range with X's impressions, likes, replies and reposts, by
  type and sortable by column. A post opens its own Post Analytics.
- **X video**: views, watch time, completion rate and average watch time, then your videos.
- **X audience**: who engaged by gender, age, country and device, for one engagement at a time.

A range picked here is X's own button pressed on X's page, so x.com shows the same range. Live
and Spaces analytics stay on x.com.
