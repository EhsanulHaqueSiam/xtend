# Analyze

Analyze is your own numbers. One paid **Refresh posts** reads your profile and up to 100 of
your own posts; every section then reads from that load. The header's freshness line says when
it was fetched and how many posts it holds, or that it is not fetched yet. The button only
stands out once the load is a day old or missing. Until the first refresh each section shows
one line and the button.

In the desktop app, **Profile on X** beside it opens your profile in the X panel for free, to
read your numbers on X itself; the paid refresh is what fills these sections.

## Overview

Followers by day, views by day published over 30 days, engagements over views, and the
headline numbers with their change.

## Posts

Your posts as a sortable table with averages by type (text, image, video, link, thread), and
the posts that gained followers. **Private metrics** (profile visits, link clicks, detail
expands) are X's own analytics, read once per own post of the last 30 days. **Fetch now** shows
its price; your first one also turns on a nightly read at 03:00 at the same price, which a switch
beside it turns off. The section
shows the last run.

## Audience

Who you see most, from the session's observations, and your followers over time.

**Themes** groups your best 50 posts by what they are about and says why each group works.
It is one model call, cached per set of posts: asking again for the same posts is free.

## Best time

A 7x24 grid of average views per hour of week in your time zone. Opacity follows the value.
It needs refreshed posts; Plan's Slots turn its best hours into posting slots.

## Competitors

Watched handles side by side with you: followers, posting rate, average views. A row opens
their top posts. Watching is free; the list is shared with Scores' Watchlist. Each row has
**Profile on X**, free, beside **Fetch**, the paid read that fills the row, with its price.

## Ask

One question, one answer, over the loaded posts summary. Suggested questions fill the box;
asking is a separate, priced click. It is not a chat: the last answer stays until the next
question.
