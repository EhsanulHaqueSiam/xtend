# Home, Today and Seen

## Home

On the desktop, Home is x.com itself, with the companion column beside it when the window is
wide (see [x.com inside Xtend](./x-view.md)). In a browser (see
[Remote service](./remote-service.md)) there is no X session, so Home opens Today.

## Today

Today is the day at a glance, first in the sidebar: what waits on you (failed posts with why,
rule approvals with their price), open Inbox items, what goes out next and today's picks, under
the week's views, engagement, followers and spend. It reads only what Xtend already holds and
costs nothing. Each row opens its tool; approvals are decided in Automate.

## Seen

Seen, in Library, is everything the X session has shown you, newest sighting first: the posts that scrolled
past on your timeline, in searches, on profiles, with the metrics X sent at the time and how
many times you saw each. It is free and builds up as you browse.

A row opens the post on X beside the tool. **Save** puts it in the Library for free, from what
the session already saw. A row drags like a post on X, onto Compose, Library, Leads or Scores
in the sidebar (see [Drag and drop](./drag-and-drop.md)). Marks under a post say what you
already did with it: replied, saved, lead, watching, auto.

Search finds posts by their text. Click an @handle to see only that author's posts; the
handle in the header clears it. Seen shows 100 posts at a time: **Older** goes further back.
Refresh pulls in what arrived since.
