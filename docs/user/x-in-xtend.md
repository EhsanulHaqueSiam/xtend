# X inside Xtend

The middle of the window is x.com, with your own login. X's left rail and right column are
hidden; Xtend's sidebar is the one navigation for X's pages and the tools, and the space on
the right holds Xtend's companion cards.

## Home and X's pages

The sidebar's first rows are X's own pages: Home, Notifications, Messages, Bookmarks and your
Profile. Notifications shows X's unread count. These open in the main area, like a browser
tab, and keep their scroll position when you leave and come back.

## X beside a tool

Open any tool and X moves to a panel on the right, with the same page and scroll. Press
`⌘\` (`Ctrl+\` on Linux) to show or hide the panel. Drag its left edge to resize it. The
panel's bar has back, forward, reload, full size, open in your browser and close.

A post you open from a tool (a mention, a saved post, a lead's latest post) opens in this
panel, so the tool stays on screen.

## Home's companion column

When the window is wide enough, Home shows five cards beside the timeline: Up next, Needs you,
Mentions, Picks today and Best time. They come from what Xtend already knows and cost nothing.
Click a card to open its tool with X kept in the panel. In a narrower window the sidebar's
Today block carries the same facts.

## What Xtend adds to X's pages

Under each post, a row with its views, engagement rate, views per hour, the author's followers,
and how the post compares with your average. Save puts the post in your Library; Reply with AI
opens Engage with a drafted reply. Hover an author's name for their follower count and a link
to their full report in Scores. In X's composer, a Schedule chip takes what you typed into
Compose.

These read the page X already rendered. Nothing is clicked, posted or fetched on your behalf.

## Clean X

Settings, X session, Clean X hides X's Grok and Premium prompts. Turn it off to see X exactly
as it ships. The hidden rail and right column are part of Xtend's layout and stay hidden.

## Reading is free, writing is yours

Xtend records the posts and numbers that scroll past in your session, which is why Analyze,
Seen and Engage fill up as you browse. The only writes through your session are the ones you
click, a reply or a post, one at a time at a human pace, and they are free. Settings has a
switch to send them through the API instead. Automations never use the session at all; they go
through X's API with your treg balance.
