# Scores

How credible an account looks, from a sample of its followers.

A report card shows the score, a tier (Unknown, Noted, Credible, Significant, Supreme), the
follower, following and post counts, when the account joined, the top followers by category
(KOLs, projects, funds, people), the share of followers that look like bots, and the score's
history.

- **Mine**: your own report.
- **Watchlist**: handles you follow over time, with their score trend. Add by typing a handle
  or by dragging a post onto Scores in the sidebar.
- **Report**: any handle. Scoring reads follower pages through X's API, so it is paid; the
  button shows the price for the number of pages.
- **Trending**: handles rising this week, by country, cached for 30 minutes.
- **Bot check**: a handle's bot share and the sample it came from.
