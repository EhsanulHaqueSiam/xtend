# x.com inside Xtend

The window is Xtend. x.com runs inside it as your own logged-in browser session: browsing it
costs nothing and looks to X like an ordinary tab. Xtend's sidebar is the navigation for both
X's pages and the tools.

## Home, Search and posts, drawn by Xtend

Home, Explore, Notifications, Bookmarks, Lists, Communities, Follow, Profile, any post you open and anyone's
profile (click their name) are Xtend's own screens. Your X session still does the
work out of sight: it loads the page you asked for and scrolls when you reach the end, so what
you see is exactly what X would show you, free. **For you** and **Following** are X's own feeds.
J and K move between posts, Enter opens one, L likes, S saves to Library. Like, repost and
bookmark press X's own buttons; quote opens Compose with the post. On your own posts the views
count (or "..." > View post analytics) opens X's Post Analytics: impressions, engagements,
detail expands, profile visits and link clicks, free. Videos play in
Xtend's player, with fullscreen and picture in picture. Search takes X's operators
(`from:`, `min_faves:`, `filter:media`), or writes them for you: the sliders button beside the
search box (or **Advanced search** in the command palette) opens X's advanced search form,
filled from the search you have open.

New posts never jump in while you read: they wait behind the blue **Show N posts**. To get them,
click Home (or the tab you are on) again, press `.`, or pull down at the top with two fingers;
with none waiting, X loads the feed fresh. ⌘R or F5 always reloads it from X. Back on Home after
ten minutes or more away, Xtend asks X quietly and keeps your place. In the desktop app, go back
from a post by swiping right with two fingers (as set in System Settings > Trackpad > Swipe
between pages), the mouse's back button, or ⌘[ (Alt+← on Windows and Linux); you land on the
same post.

Bookmark folders are X Premium's. With Premium, click the Bookmarks title for your folders;
Create Folder, Edit Folder and Delete Folder open X's own dialogs, and a post's share menu has
Bookmark to Folder (and, inside a folder, Remove from Folder). Without Premium the title shows
X's offer, and Upgrade opens X's own page.

**Articles** (X Premium) is X's long-form editor, drawn by Xtend: X's toolbar, the 5:2 cover,
the title and the body, with X's limits checked beside it, assist, and your other article
drafts. **Publish** offers three ways out, each with its price: **Publish now** fills X's own
editor once and presses X's Publish for your click, free, with who can reply and a caption set in
X's dialog; **Queue** and **Schedule** publish later through the X API ($0.01: a draft and a
publish request). The X API cannot carry a cover or a reply setting yet, and X's editor takes
code, LaTeX, dividers and embedded posts only from its own Insert menu, so each way out says what
it cannot carry before you press it; when X's editor cannot take the article, Publish offers the X
API instead, at its price. Articles you started on x.com are listed and open read-only;
**Copy to Xtend** makes a draft of one, and says first what it leaves out (the cover, images,
embedded posts, dividers, LaTeX). Without Premium, Articles shows X's offer, and **Upgrade to
unlock** opens X's own page. Writing works in a browser too; Publish now needs the desktop app.

An NBA team's profile shows its schedule as X does. A game opens with the day's scores, the
score, clock and last play, and X's Top and Latest posts; Highlights, Stats, Plays and Chat open
X's own game page in the panel. **NBA** on a game opens the league page: the day's games, the
standings by conference, and the stat leaders for the stat you pick.

A news row in Explore opens its story: X's headline, when X last updated it, Grok's summary with
X's caveat, and the story's posts in X's tabs (Top, Latest). Saving, reporting and sharing a story
stay on x.com.

The box at the top of Home is X's composer: images or a video, a poll, who can reply, emoji
(the system picker, on macOS and Windows) and @mentions suggested from people Xtend has already
seen on X. Post goes through your session, free, and X's own composer sets the poll and who can
reply. With session actions off, in a browser without the desktop app, or over 280 characters,
the box moves to Compose as a draft, where the price is shown.
The box at the top of Home is X's composer: images or a video, a GIF, a poll, who can reply,
emoji (the system picker, on macOS and Windows) and @mentions suggested from people Xtend has
already seen on X. Post goes through your session, free, and X's own composer sets the GIF, the
poll and who can reply. GIFs come from X's own GIF search, so the GIF button shows only while
session actions are on in the desktop app, and Compose cannot take a GIF. With session actions
off, in a browser without the desktop app, or over 280 characters, the box moves to Compose as a
draft, where the price is shown.

**Choose audience** (it reads "Everyone") posts to one of your Communities instead, as on X: the
list is X's own, in X's order, and once you pick one, "Also share with followers" shares it beyond
the Community too. It goes through your session, free, as only X's composer posts to a Community,
so it shows only while session actions are on. A Community decides who can reply to its posts.

**Bold** and **Italic** format the text you select (⌘B and ⌘I, Ctrl on Windows and Linux) when
your X account has Premium, as on X. Only X's own composer applies them, so they show only while
session actions are on, and a formatted post goes through your session, free. On an account
without Premium they show X's own offer, which opens X's Subscribe page. Posts written in bold or
italic on X show that way in Xtend.

Replying starts by reading the post: its text, its images and the thread above it. Xtend says
what it thinks the post is ("Playful hot take") and why; if that is wrong, say what it is
instead. **Angles** are replies written for that post, **With help** reworks your own words
and keeps your voice, **Your own** has no AI at all. A reply goes through your session, free;
the reading runs on your own Claude or Codex login.

## The sidebar

The X rows at the top open X's pages: Home, Explore, Notifications, Bookmarks, Lists, Communities,
Follow, Profile and Chat in Xtend. Chat reads your conversations from X's own page and opens one
only when you click it, since X then marks it read. What you write, react, edit or delete there
goes through X's own controls in your session, free; what X asks next (a delete's confirm, the
forward picker, a request's Delete) shows X's page for you to answer; so does the first message
of a new chat. More in a conversation
opens X's page about it, for nicknames, disappearing messages, calls, the safety number and
blocking. When X asks for your chat passcode, X's page shows for you to type it. A Community's Join presses
X's own button; one that asks first opens X's own rules dialog, where you agree and ask. Its Pin
presses X's Pin too; the Community shows as a tab on Home once X's Home loads again (⌘R there).
Follow is X's Who to follow and
Creators for you; a creator's Subscribe opens X's own subscribe dialog, where X shows the price. Notifications shows X's unread count. The tools follow: Compose, Plan,
Library, Engage, Leads, Automate, Analyze, Scores, Seen, then Settings. Each tool's sections
show as rows under it once it is open. Compose counts queued drafts and Engage counts open
mentions.

Hover a tool for one line on what it does. The first time you open a tool, that line and the
open section's line sit under the header; × closes them for good and the ⓘ by the title brings
them back.

At the foot, the Today block shows the next queued post, how many are queued, this month's
spend against the budget, and whether the service is local, remote or offline. It opens
Settings.

## X beside a tool

On a tool, x.com docks as a panel on the right, so a post you are replying to or a profile you
are scoring stays in view. A post or profile you open from a tool (a mention, a saved post, a
lead) opens in this panel and the tool stays on screen. The panel bar has back, forward,
reload, pin, full size, open in your browser and close. Drag its left edge to resize it. In a narrow window the sidebar becomes an icon rail and
the panel narrows to leave room for the tool; your preferred panel width returns when the window grows.

Pinned, the sidebar's X rows load into the panel and the open tool stays. Unpinned, they take
the main area.

Use the panel toggle in any tool header or `⌘\` to show or hide X. From X itself it returns you to the last tool with the panel open.
Closing the panel parks it; your place on X and your scroll position survive.

## The companion column

While X fills the main area and the window is wide enough, five cards sit where X's right
column was: Up next, Needs you, Inbox, Picks today and Best time (Good time now when your
audience usually peaks about now). They come from what Xtend already holds and cost nothing.
Each opens its tool, with X in the panel beside it unless you closed the panel; Best time
starts a new draft. In a narrower window the sidebar's Today block carries the same facts.

## Clean X

Settings, X session, has a **Clean X** group: one switch per part of x.com to hide. CSS only:
X's own code runs untouched and nothing is clicked or fetched on your behalf. Changes apply at
once and are a preference of this window.

| switch                                           | default |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| Hide ads                                         | on      |
| Hide Grok                                        | on      |
| Hide Premium upsells                             | on      |
| Hide X's nav rail and right column               | on      |
| Hide Discover more and More posts under a thread | on      |
| Hide reply boosts and Subscribe                  | on      |
| Hide Communities, Jobs and Spaces                | on      |
| Hide the For you tab                             | off     |
| Hide view counts                                 | off     |
| Hide verified badges                             | off     |
| Quiet media until hover                          | off     |

**Restore defaults** on that page puts these back.

## What Xtend adds on top of X's pages

Three small pieces sit on X's pages, drawn from what X already rendered, never from a request:

- **Metrics row** under each post: views, engagement rate, views per hour, the author's
  followers and how the post compares to your average. Two actions: Save to the Library and
  Reply, which opens a chooser under the post: write your own, or pick a tone for three drafts.
  The reply goes out the same way as a reply sent from Engage.
- **Schedule chip** in X's composer, before Post. Click it and what you typed moves into
  Compose, where it can be queued for a slot.
- **Hover card** on an author's avatar or name after a short pause: their followers and a
  Full report link that opens Scores on them.

The metrics row also carries marks for what you already did with the post or its author:
`replied` with when, `saved`, `lead` with their stage, `watching`, and `auto` with the rule
that acted on it. The same marks show in Seen and on Engage's posts. They are read from your
own data; nothing is fetched. A mark appears as soon as you Save.

`⌥O` hides and shows the metrics rows.

## What the app reads from your session

As you browse, the app reads the posts, authors and metrics that X sent to the page and stores
them in the service. That is where the Seen feed, the free parts of Engage and Analyze, and
the best-time suggestions come from. Nothing is replayed to X. Public posts can also go to the
shared pool behind Library's Trends while pool sharing is on (see [Settings](./settings.md)).

## Acting through the session

What you click can go through your own session, free. **Act through X for what I click**
(Settings, X session) is on by default:

- Post now, and the replies, likes and DMs you send from Engage and Leads, go through your
  session, one at a time at a human pace: at least 8 seconds apart and at most 30 an hour. A
  click the session cannot take right then offers **Try again**, free, or the API now at its
  price. Off, they go through the X API at the price on the button.
- Posts with media always go through your session, since the API path has no media upload. A
  queued one waits at its time for your **Post now**; with the switch off it says session
  actions are off.

Queued posts without media go through the X API at its price. Automations and agents never use
the session.

## Links

x.com links stay inside the window. Google and Apple sign-in open a popup on the same session.
Any other site opens in your default browser.
