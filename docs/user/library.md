# Library

The Library is posts worth learning from: today's picks for your keywords, X search, an
archive of everything the two have seen, trends, and what you saved. Every list is cards in a
masonry; a click opens the post in the detail column with its full text and every number.
Hover a card for **Remix** (Compose, with this post beside the editor) and **Save**. The arrow
opens the post on X beside the tool. A card also drags like a post on X: onto a sidebar row
(see [Drag and drop](./drag-and-drop.md)) or onto a collection.

Picks and Archive carry a format filter (media, text, links, and threads in the Archive), a
sort, and **Search X**, priced as you type: submitting opens Search with the query. `s`
anywhere opens Picks with Search X focused.

## Picks

Today's top posts for your keywords (Settings), and the staff picks under them: curated
evergreen posts whose text arrives with one search per author. The selected pick offers **Why
it works** and a **ready-to-post version** in your voice, each a model call. Ready to post,
Archive's **Write mine in this format** and Trends' post ideas take a tone, or any, and offer
three to pick from; the one you send lands in Compose as a suggested draft.

The picks are gathered by a morning run that spends treg with nobody clicking, so it is off
until **Daily Library jobs** is turned on in Settings. The section says when today's picks
are in. The jobs also write **three posts for today** in your voice from your For you topics
and the picks, filed under Compose's Automatic section.

## Search

**Top** and **Latest** in the header pick X's sort. X's own search syntax works here
(`from:`, `min_faves:`, `since:`).

The free way first: **Search on X** opens X's own search for the query in the panel, and the
session files what it shows. Posts the session has already seen that hold every word of the
query list on top, marked seen on X and free.

**Search** is X search through treg, for volume and filters. Every run and every next page
shows its price on the button. Excluded words filter the results after they arrive, so they
cost nothing. Results are cached for a while; the same query again within that window is
free.

## Archive

Every post a search or a pick has seen, in the chosen order, with the model's labels for its
format and its hook pattern. Filter by format, minimum views, year, author size, language and
hook. **Find in archive** matches the wording, author handle or name of posts already archived,
free; **Search X** in the header is the paid read of X itself. **Detect hooks** labels the next 20 unlabelled posts in one model call; the selected
post offers **Write mine in this format**, a model call that drafts your version.

## Trends

**For you** comes first: topics ranked on your machine against what you post, your keywords,
your saved posts and the accounts you reply to, each with the reason it is there. They come
from phrases several accounts in your X session posted today and words rising in the shared
pool; one row is kept for a topic outside your usual lane. Free, and nothing about you leaves
the app. **Write 3 for today** drafts posts from them into Compose's Automatic section.

Then the pool: what everyone's X is doing, free, read from the shared pool of public
posts that installs with sharing on send in. Two tables: posts gaining the most views in the
last 24 hours (with likes and how many installs saw them), and words rising against the week
before. The line over them says how many posts and installs it holds and when it was read;
**Refresh** reads it again, free. It stays empty until three installs have shared for a day.
It reads the same with your own sharing off (see [Settings](./settings.md)).

Under it, X's trending topics by country through treg, cached for 30 minutes, with post ideas
the model ties to the trends that fit your keywords. Nothing loads until you click
**Load trends**, since the read is paid.

## Collections and Swipe file

Save puts a post in the swipe file, with its metrics frozen at save time. A post can also sit
in any number of named collections; name one in the Collections rail to start it, rename it
inline, delete it after a confirm. Drag a card onto a collection in the rail to file it there
too, or check several cards and use **Add to collection** to file them together. Staff picks
fills itself and takes no drop. The selected saved post takes a note, more
collections and remix. Inside a collection, Remove takes the post out of that collection; in
the swipe file, Delete saved post removes it everywhere, after a confirm. The swipe file is
searchable by text and note.

The swipe file, each collection and the Archive show 200 posts at a time with the full count;
**Show more** loads the next 200, up to 1,000 on screen. Past that, search or filter to find
the rest.
