# Library

The Library is posts worth learning from: today's picks for your keywords, paid X search, an
archive of everything the two have seen, trends, and what you saved. Every list is cards in a
masonry; a click opens the post in the detail column with its full text and every number.
Hover a card for **Remix** (Compose, with this post beside the editor) and **Save**. The arrow
opens the post on X beside the tool.

The header of each section carries a format filter (text, image, video, link, thread), a sort,
and **Search X**, priced as you type: submitting opens Search with the query. `s` focuses it.

## Picks

Today's top posts for your keywords (Settings), and the staff picks under them: curated
evergreen posts whose text arrives with one search per author. The selected pick offers **Why
it works** and a **ready-to-post version** in your voice, each a priced model call.

The picks are gathered by a morning run that spends treg with nobody clicking, so it is off
until **Daily Library jobs** is turned on in Settings. The section says when today's picks
are in. The jobs also write **three posts for today** in your voice from the trends and the
picks, filed under Compose's Automatic section.

## Search

X search through treg. X's own search syntax works here (`from:`, `min_faves:`, `since:`).
Every run and every next page shows its price on the button. Excluded words filter the results
after they arrive, so they cost nothing. Results are cached for a while; the same query again
within that window is free.

## Archive

Every post a search or a pick has seen, up to the latest 200, with the model's labels for its
format and its hook pattern. Filter by format, hook, keyword and date. **Label the next 20**
runs the labeller over unlabelled posts; the selected post offers **Write mine in this
format**, a priced model call that drafts your version.

## Trends

X's trending topics by country, cached for 30 minutes, with post ideas the model ties to the
trends that fit your keywords. The country list is the same one Scores' Trending uses. Nothing
loads until you click Load, since the read is paid.

## Collections and Swipe file

Save puts a post in the swipe file, with its metrics frozen at save time. A post can also sit
in any number of named collections; name one in the Collections rail to start it, rename it
inline, delete it after a confirm. The selected saved post takes a note, more collections,
remix and remove. Remove inside a collection takes the post out of that collection; in the
swipe file it deletes the saved post everywhere. The swipe file is searchable by text and note.
